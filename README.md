# Hive Mind

**Want to start with AI, but don't know where to begin? Begin here.**

Think of Hive Mind as a starter pack for AI, with a major upgrade. The starter pack: it sets you up with Claude, the AI made by Anthropic, one safe step at a time, so you are never left wondering what to do next. The upgrade: once it is set up, your Claude remembers you, keeps your things in order, and asks you before anything big.

This is a fresh copy. It has no notes yet. They start with your first conversation.

## Why not just open Claude and start?

You can, and Claude is good on its own. But starting alone is hard in a quiet way. Every new chat begins from nothing, so you explain who you are and what you are doing, again and again. You are not sure what to ask, what it can do for you, or what is safe to say yes to.

With Hive Mind you do not start alone. Your first hour is a walk, not a puzzle: each step says what to do, what you should see after it, and what to do if you see something else. On Windows you never type a command yourself. Claude types it and asks you first.

After that, every new chat already knows you: who you are, what you are working on, what you decided and what comes next. You say a thing once. When you correct it, the correction stays, and you do not have to say it again next week.

When you find something worth keeping, a link or a file, you send it and say "keep this". Claude reads all of it and puts the useful parts where they belong, so good finds stop getting lost. When you want Claude to work a new way, or to stop doing something, you say it in one sentence.

And you stay in charge. It never spends money, deletes anything, sends or publishes anything without your yes. Everything it knows about you sits in plain files in your own private copy on GitHub, which only you can read, and which you can open and change yourself.

Hive Mind does not replace anything Claude already gives you: chats on your computer and in the cloud, skills, tools it can connect to, scheduled runs and a phone app. It adds what Claude does not have, the memory and the guidance around it. [What Claude already does](docs/claude-app.md) shows both side by side.

## Who it is for

Anyone who wants to start with AI and does not know where to begin. And anyone who already uses Claude for real work and a full life, and is tired of explaining themselves again at the start of every chat. You do not need to know anything about programming.

## What you need

- **Claude Pro or higher.** The free plan does not include Claude Code, the part of Claude that works with files on your computer, which Hive Mind runs on. (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise all work.)
- **The Claude desktop app** for Windows or Mac. A Linux version exists too, still marked as a test version by Anthropic.
- **A free GitHub account.** GitHub is a website that keeps files online. It stores your notes in a private copy that only you can read.
- **About one hour.** Claude installs the two free programs Hive Mind needs (Git and Node) for you, after your yes.
