# What you sent

Everything you send to keep is listed here the moment it arrives: a link, a file, a screenshot, a video. Claude reads all of it, files the useful parts, and then ticks the line and writes where the parts went. An open line means something about it is still owed.

A line reads: `- [ ] when | what it is | the link, or the file and where it is kept | waiting, or what happened and where it went`
