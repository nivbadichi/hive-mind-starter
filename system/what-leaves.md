# What leaves your computer

The one home for this list. Your page shows it. Add a line here before anything new is connected.

| What | Where it goes | When |
|---|---|---|
| Your notes | GitHub, your own private copy | When you save, after the privacy check |
| What you and Claude say in a chat, and the files that chat opens | Anthropic | While a chat is open |
| Pages a chat opens to look something up | The site that is opened | Only when a chat looks something up |
| The names and versions of the parts installed for Hive Mind (nothing of your notes) | The public security lists of npm and GitHub | Once a day, from the daily watchdog, once the parts are installed; say "watchdog off" to stop it |
| A request for a project's base box (Docker): nothing of yours is sent, the box is downloaded | Docker's own store online | Only if a project with code runs in Docker, at its first build, after your yes |

Stays on your computer: the list of private names, your sign-in details, and Claude's own logs.
