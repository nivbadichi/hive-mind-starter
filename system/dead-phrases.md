# Phrases that are no longer true

When a decision replaces something, the old words tend to stay behind in a note. Each line below is such a phrase. The health check searches your current notes for them and flags any it finds (lines that speak of the past, with words like "dropped", "was" or "replaced", are let through).

Claude adds a line here in the same piece of work that makes a phrase untrue. A line reads: `- "the phrase" | what is true now | since when`
