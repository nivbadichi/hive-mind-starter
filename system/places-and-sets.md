# Your places and sets

Filled in by the first conversation. Change it any time by telling Claude.

## Places

The default pack of four. It comes with every Hive Mind and cannot shrink: Today, Initiative, Life and System are never removed or renamed.

- Today: only what needs you today.
- Initiative: ideas you didn't have to ask for.
- Life: every project, with its next step.
- System: one look tells you it's all working.

Your own places come after the four, up to eight places in all. Add one by telling Claude ("Add a place for my garden"). Rename, split or remove only the ones you added; removing one sends its projects back to Life, and nothing is deleted. Past eight, Hive Mind suggests grouping a few.

## Sets

- Daily brief: off
- Initiative: off
- Connections: off. Nothing connects until you say yes.
